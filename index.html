How it works

Four steps from request to delivery. We never buy anything until you approve the quote.

  1. Send your requestDescribe the item, how many you want, your budget and where to deliver.
  2. We send a quoteThe quote shows the item cost, our service fee and shipping.
  3. You approve and payWe purchase after you say yes and your payment is confirmed.
  4. We buy and shipWe send you the shipping details as soon as your order is on its way.
